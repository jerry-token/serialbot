# SerialBot name and logo

The code of SerialBot is licensed under the GPL-3.0 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)
and [NOTICE](NOTICE)). The GPL covers the code only: it does not give
permission to use the name **SerialBot** or its logo.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying that your project uses, works with, or is based on SerialBot
  ("based on SerialBot by Jerry, https://github.com/jerry-token/serialbot").
- Unmodified copies of an official release, under the name SerialBot.
- Articles, tutorials, videos and reviews about SerialBot.

## Not allowed

- Publishing a modified version, a fork or a rebuild under the name
  SerialBot or with its logo, or in a way that suggests it is the official
  project or comes from its author.
- Copying the project under another name without saying where it came
  from (the GPL also requires keeping the copyright notices and marking
  your changes; see NOTICE).
- Using the name or logo for other products, domains or accounts.

A fork is welcome under its own name, with a clear note that it is based on
SerialBot.
